## Read this first
The demo is dynamic in a decorative way (grid, glows, orbit rings) but says little. ## 1. The story, top to bottom [OPINION unless cited]

Company facts: marketplace connecting students with independent counselors, California first, then US [FACT: CLAUDE.md]; student demand first; guides are the growth lever [FACT: CLAUDE.md, DECK]; "Matched, not assigned" [FACT: BIOS]; "Good guidance was never scarce, access to it was" [FACT: LI, GFX post 3].

Arc: (1) The process is confusing and the help is unevenly available. (2) Here is clear, sourced help right now, free. (3) Here is what we are building: a way to find a counselor who fits, with the reason shown. (4) Tell us what you need.

- Student: should feel "I can understand this, and someone is on my side." Do: read the UC or paying-for-college guide now, join waitlist.
- Parent: should feel "these people are straight with me." Do: read the how-to-choose-a-counselor guide, which even discloses D2Excel's stake [FACT: ART-COUNSELOR], then waitlist. Parents are often the payer [FACT: AUD], so they need their own entry on Home, not just a nav item.

## 3. Motion that means something [OPINION]

Principle: every animation should either teach a step or reinforce one idea (fit). No motion for its own sake.

1. The journey line. A single line runs the page as you scroll, passing through nodes: Explore (guides) > Apply (deadlines, UC window) > Decide > Get help > Accept. Each guide card lights its node. It makes the whole page one path, and "applications to acceptance" is the student's real story. Keep the final node as an open, unlabeled endpoint, not a promise of acceptance.
2. The match, as two orbits. The logo is a blue ellipse around "D2E", and a variant has an orbit ring [FACT: brief 5.1]. Show two bodies (a student dot in blue, a counselor dot in red, the logo's two colors [FACT: brief 5.1]) on separate orbits that drift and then lock into one shared orbit as the user scrolls past "Matched, not assigned". Existing demo already has a blue and a red dot; give them a narrative. Label it clearly as "the idea" so it does not read as a live matcher (website-plan 5.3 forbids implying a feature that does not exist).
3. The "2". The brand name is "Desire to Excel" [FACT: DECK, STRAT, PRD]. Let the red "2" in the hero be the one thing that moves: Desire [2] Excel, with the 2 rising or bridging the two words as the headline lands, then settling. Needs a decision on spelling (question 3). Keep only one red moment per page.
4. California. A quiet card, not a map animation: a simple outline with a pin that pulses once when scrolled in, tied to the "launching in California" line. Cheap, and a neat place to say "Bay Area first" if confirmed.
5. Deadline moment. A live-feeling countdown to the real UC deadline (Nov 30) on the UC guide card. It is true, urgent, and useful, and it is a date, not an invented statistic. Computed from the calendar, no data needed.
6. Reveal the "reason": a counselor card flips to show "why this fit" using obviously illustrative sample text marked as a sample. Only if Gurashish accepts the label; skip otherwise.

## 5. Guardrails

- No invented statistics, testimonials, counts, partner logos, press, ratings. None exist [FACT: BIOS, AUD]. ARCH itself puts the numbers section on hold [FACT: ARCH].
- No admissions or aid guarantees. D2Excel's own counselor article lists "we guarantee admission" as a red flag [FACT: ART-COUNSELOR]. A journey animation ending in "Accepted" would imply the opposite, so avoid a final acceptance graphic.
- Caseload statistic: 376 (LI/GFX), 400:1 (DECK) and 195-224 (ART-COUNSELOR) conflict. Use none until Gurashish picks one sourced number.
- "AI-powered matching": matching is manual in Phase 2, algorithmic in Phase 3 [FACT: PRD, DECK]. Do not say it. "Vetted counselors" and "counselor review": gating (background check, insurance) is not live [FACT: STRAT, PRD]. Say "we are building" or "planned".
- Minors: PRD blocks sign-up under 13; California restricts sale or sharing of under-16 data without opt-in [FACT: AUD via brief]; parental-consent rule is contradictory between STRAT and PRD Rev 4. So: no animations that collect anything, no cursor or behavior tracking beyond consented analytics, no language that targets young children, no "enter your age/grade" gimmicks on Home, and a 13-or-older-or-parent line on the form. Needs legal input.
- Social Hub / open chat for teens: do not tease it (conflicts with private-by-default posture [FACT: PRD, STRAT]).
- Photos: do not use existing stock-style social-graphic photos until licensing is confirmed [FACT: brief 5.1].
- Maximize "reduced motion" and "Pause motion" support already built; keep them (WCAG).

Contradictions on the current page
- Hero line "Find the counselor who fits where you're headed" plus a "Meet" step: promises a finder that does not exist. It is softened by "Coming soon" and "none exist yet" below, but the H1 alone, which is what a share preview and Google show, reads as live. Fix via headline C.
- Meta description: "D2Excel is a marketplace connecting students with college admissions counselors": consistent with CLAUDE.md and ART-COUNSELOR, but ARCH/POS disagree on whether we lead with "free workspace" vs "marketplace" (open question). Not a contradiction with documents, a gap.
- Page has no parent entry; PERSONA priority-1 is a parent [FACT: AUD]. Gap, not contradiction.
- "Written with California students in mind": the research is Bay Area focused [FACT: POS, AUD]; the six guides are California. Fine, but see question 2.
- Fonts and palette: demo uses Bricolage Grotesque and a cobalt gradient family. There is no brand guide [FACT: brief 5.1]. Not a contradiction, just undecided.
- Live d2excel.com says "An AI Powered Student Success Platform" [OBS: website-plan 0]. If both go live around the same time, the old claim contradicts the new site. Cutover removes it.
