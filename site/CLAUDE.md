# D2Excel site

Astro static site (demo). Owned by Forge for implementation; company facts come from `../website-brief.md` and `../CLAUDE.md`.

**Read before front-end work:** load the `frontend-toolbox` skill. It lists every installed design, motion, audit, and testing tool and the order to use them.

**Stack:** Astro, CSS (scroll-driven animations, sticky, 3D transforms) with a small vanilla script on redesigned pages, GSAP (core and Flip) only for the logo reveal. The pages not yet redesigned also use GSAP ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVG, Lenis, and cross-document View Transitions.

**Where things are:** the home page is `src/pages/index.astro` on `SystemLayout` with `src/styles/system/home.css` (redesigned, direction C). The guides hub and article template (`src/pages/guides/`, `guides.css`, `article.css`) are redesigned too and read the `guides` content collection (`src/content.config.ts`; front matter exported from Drive by Pioneer; bodies are company text and must not be edited; `<!-- INTERNAL -->` notes are stripped by `src/plugins/guide-article.mjs`, which needs `@astrojs/markdown-remark` and `markdown.processor: unified(...)` in `astro.config.mjs`). Guide order, category tiles and reading time live in `src/config.ts`. For students and For counselors are redesigned too, on the shared inner-page template: `src/layouts/InnerLayout.astro` (SystemLayout plus `src/styles/system/inner.css`) and `src/components/system/` `InnerHero`, `InnerSection`, `FeatureRow`, `LiveBoard`, `InnerAsk` (How it works and About use it from round 2; Join from round 3 with `src/styles/system/join.css` and `src/scripts/join.ts`, and `inner={false}` to skip `inner.ts`; see DESIGN.md). `InnerLayout` also adds WebPage and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD from `title` and `description` (`pageLd` in `config.ts`). The other pages (Privacy, Terms, 404) still use `Layout.astro`, `src/components/` and `src/styles/` (`base`, `chrome`, `hero`, `sections`, `motion`, `intro`; `global.css` only imports them) until their round. Scripts in `src/scripts/`: `system.ts` (redesigned pages: menu, dock (shown after the first scroll), wall cursor, Pacific time, focus handling, starts the logo reveal), `main.ts` and `motion.ts` (older pages; `motion.ts` only when motion is allowed), `intro.ts` (second half of the logo reveal, shared). Logo: `public/logo.png` (original) and `public/logo-tight.png` (cropped, used in the UI).

**Logo reveal:** set up by the head script in `Layout.astro` or `SystemLayout.astro` (`html.intro`). Home passes `intro="arrival"` to `SystemLayout`: it plays on every fresh arrival (a direct load, a reload, a new tab, a visit from another site) and is skipped only when the visitor comes from another page of this site (same-origin referrer, including the logo link). Every other page keeps the old rule, once per session (`sessionStorage` key `d2e-intro`, which Home also sets). Phase 1 is CSS (`intro.css`: the plate rises, a blue and a red arc draw round a larger ring in opposite directions and meet at the bottom, the logo's ring draws, the D2E wipes in, the 2 pulses red), phase 2 is `intro.ts` (GSAP Flip into the header logo). About 2.1 s. Skippable by click, tap or any key; never shown for reduced motion or without JS; a 6s CSS failsafe hides it. On redesigned pages anything with class `arrive` waits for the curtain to open (CSS, `animation-play-state`). Never recolor or redraw the logo; the SVG ring is a decorative stroke that matches it. Harness scripts that need the page without the reveal must send a same-origin `referer` (Playwright `goto(url, { referer })`); setting the session key no longer skips it on Home.

**Scroll-driven CSS and the build:** the production minifier folds `animation` plus `animation-timeline` into one shorthand that Chromium 145 drops, so a scroll-driven animation can work in `astro dev` and do nothing in the build. Write the longhands (`animation-name`, `animation-timing-function`, `animation-fill-mode`, `animation-timeline`, `animation-range`) for anything driven by `scroll()` or `view()`, and check `getComputedStyle(el).animationName` on the production build. Still affected elsewhere (not fixed, outside the Home round): `.fit` in `inner.css` (How it works) and `.wall-col` in `hero.css`.

**Hard rules:**
- All text visible and readable at all times; contrast at WCAG AA or better; no clipping or overlap.
- Motion is scroll- or cursor-driven, or settles within about 5 seconds. Nothing loops or runs longer on its own, so no Pause motion button is needed (WCAG 2.2.2). Reduced motion gets a real static fallback, and a page with JavaScript off is complete and readable.
- Budgets, measured on the production build: mobile Lighthouse performance 95 or higher, CLS 0, LCP within 2.5s on the throttled (simulated) run. JavaScript about 80 kB gzipped for home and about 40 kB for inner pages.
- Don't lose speed: re-measure Lighthouse (mobile and desktop), LCP and CLS on the production build after changes, and compare with `QA-LOG.md` (baseline of 2026-10-10: home mobile 97, desktop 99, LCP 2.3s, CLS 0; redesigned home 2026-10-10: mobile 99, desktop 100, LCP 2.1s, CLS 0, first-visit JS 40 kB; Home after round 4: see QA-LOG section 11).
- No pricing, fees, or payout details on the site yet. No invented statistics, testimonials, or claims. Avoid "AI-powered", "vetted", "counselor review", and caseload numbers.
- Launch wording: the site presents itself as California (launching in California, expanding across the US). Do not say "Bay Area first" in copy or design.
- Local only: no deploy, no push.

**Design system (redesign in progress):** direction C, Hybrid. Tokens and components are in `src/styles/system/` and documented in `DESIGN.md`. A private page showing every token and component in dark and light sections is at `/lab/system/` (local only: noindex, outside the sitemap, linked from nowhere, and built only in dev or with `npm run build:lab`; its sources are in `src/lab/`). Existing pages still use the older styles until their redesign round.
