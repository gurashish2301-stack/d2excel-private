## B. Scope notes that prevent a public mistake
- **195 to 224** is the range for high schools only (grades 9 to 12) among the 35 states that report grade levels separately. It is not a national average and not California.
- **432** is California, all grades (5,805,510 students, 13,436 counselors), 8th-highest by Scout's reading. 432 and 195 to 224 measure different scopes; don't set one against the other.
