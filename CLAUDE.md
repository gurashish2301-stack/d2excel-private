# D2EXCEL

Work for D2XL (D2Excel) — a two-sided marketplace connecting students with college admissions counselors. Launching in California, with plans to expand across the US. Owned by the `pioneer` subagent (see `~/.claude/agents/pioneer.md`), which has read/limited-write access to the D2Excel work Google Drive (`gurashish@d2excel.com`).

## Business model
- **Marketplace:** students on one side, counselors on the other. Student-side demand has to come first — a marketplace with counselors but no students (or vice versa) doesn't work.
- **Growth lever:** to build the student base before/alongside counselor supply, D2Excel is publishing SEO content in the college-admissions domain — application guides, entrance exam info (SAT/ACT), how to pick a major, financial aid, etc. This is top-of-funnel content marketing, not the product itself.
- The `Marketing/SEO Website Content` Drive folder (application guides, SAT/ACT, major selection, college money) is this content effort — confirmed as D2Excel's own content, not a client's.
- **Status (2026-10-08):** early/raw. Gurashish is doing high-level Claude Code setup now; the content strategy itself will get more attention later.
