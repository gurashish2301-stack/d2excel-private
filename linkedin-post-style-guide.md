# D2Excel LinkedIn posts: what posts 1 to 3 do (style guide to reproduce)

Observed 2026-10-12 from `1-3 LinkedIn Posts Draft.docx` and the three graphics (local copies in `~/Documents/Desire2Excel/Social Media/`). The earlier plain drafts (`linkedin-post-drafts.md`) were rejected: too cautious, no graphic, no punch. This guide says what to copy instead.

## The text formula (about 130 to 190 words, no hashtags, no link, no emoji)
1. **Cold-open hook, one line.** A number or a blunt claim: "The average public school counselor is responsible for 376 students." / "Nobody talks about the real price of bad college guidance until it's already been paid." / "Here's what nobody says out loud: ..."
2. **The number in context.** One or two sentences that make the stat feel real (recommended 250:1, actual 50% above, 1 in 4 schools with none).
3. **A concrete picture.** What it means for a family: the course schedule, college list, aid forms, letters, split across a caseload in a 180-day year. Or a student who picks a school on name, transfers, loses credits.
4. **The reframe, always the same move: "This isn't a ___ problem. It's a ___ problem."** (Not a counselor problem, a structural math problem. Not kids who can't commit, decisions without information.) It is kind to everyone involved, never blaming.
5. **D2Excel as the answer, one sentence:** "D2Excel is building / exists to close that gap: an open marketplace connecting students and families to a wider range of counselors, plus decision-support information that isn't gated behind who can afford it."
6. **Source line last:** "Source: ASCA 2024-25 ratio data, as reported by K-12 Dive, Feb 2026."
Recurring phrases worth keeping as brand voice: "structural gap", "good guidance was never scarce, access to it was", "right counselor match", "not gated behind who can already afford it".

## What to fix when reproducing (so the posts survive scrutiny)
- Every number must be re-verified at source (the old three conflict with newer Drive documents): 376 per counselor, "1 in 4 high schools has no counselor", "1 in 3 transfer".
- "Counsellor" in post 1's header should be "Counselor".
- Contrast: white text on pale green (post 1) fails; use dark text or a darker background.
- Remove "Find Your Counselor Match Today" and "Get Started Today" unless the page behind them exists. The live d2excel.com says something different (see `linkedin-post-drafts.md` section 0).
- Photos: the people photos look generated or stock; confirm license or generate fresh, clearly licensed images.
- Keep present tense honest but confident: "D2Excel is building an open marketplace..." reads as strong as "is an open marketplace" and is true.
